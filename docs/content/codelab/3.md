Start Grow's built-in development server from inside your pod directory:

```bash
grow run
```

Grow loads your pod and starts a local preview server:

```text
  Grow v2.2.3  ready in 0.14s

  ➜  Local:    http://localhost:8080/
  ➜  Console:  http://localhost:8080/_grow/
  ➜  Pod:      /path/to/examples/starter
```

Open `http://localhost:8080/` in your browser to preview your site, and visit `http://localhost:8080/_grow/` to open the **Dev Console**, where you can search routes, filter pages and assets, and inspect localized URLs.

Changes to files in `content/`, `views/`, `static/`, or `podspec.yaml` are reflected immediately on page refresh.
